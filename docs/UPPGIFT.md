Hacking Healthcare: Rules & Inspiration
________________________________________
Important: You need no medical background, and Everi Labs and the mentors will help you sharpen your idea during the day. Below is some inspiration on what could be built, but you may bring your own problem and/or solution to the hackathon. 
________________________________________
Ground rules
No code written prior to the hackathon. All code should be written on-site.
Download before Saturday. If you’re using a dataset: some files are several gigabytes, and venue wifi is shared by 80 people.
No real patient data. Use only open or synthetic data above, or data you generate yourself.
Check the license before you build on a dataset. Some, like Medical Appointments No Show, allow non-commercial use only, and most ask you to cite the source.

1. Show the receipts
Doctors spend hours writing clinical notes after their visits. Build an AI that drafts the note from the doctor-patient conversation, where every sentence links back to the exact moment in the conversation it came from. It could also flag what is missing, such as a symptom nobody asked about.
Data
ACI-Bench: 207 role-played doctor-patient conversations, each paired with a clinical note. It is the largest open dataset for this task. It includes both human and automatic speech-recognition transcripts, so you can test how your system copes with transcription errors. (paper)
